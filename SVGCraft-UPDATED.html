A workspace that stays out of your way.

SVGCraft gives you the essential tools without burying your canvas under complicated menus. Start with a shape, build it up, and fine-tune the details whenever inspiration strikes.

Build from simple shapes

Start with rectangles, circles, stars, triangles, lines, and text. Combine simple elements into artwork that feels entirely yours.

Fine-tune every detail

Use the properties inspector to adjust colors, outlines, dimensions, position, transparency, and rotation with precision.

Work visually

Move objects directly on the canvas, resize them with handles, and keep track of your design with the layers panel.

SVG keeps your artwork crisp at any size, making it useful for web graphics, icons, and other creative projects.

From blank canvas to finished idea

Your workflow.
Your way.

Create a shape, select it, then use the inspector to make it yours. Drag objects around the artboard and use the corner handles to change their size. The canvas is the workspace, not a puzzle to solve.

Choose a tool and add an object. Select it to reveal its controls. When the design feels right, export your SVG and take it wherever you need it.

Move objects by dragging them on the canvas. Resize, rotate, and arrange your artwork visually.
